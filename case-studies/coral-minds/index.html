Coral Minds — a coaching institute website built for clarity.

A website for a coaching institute where parents and students form their first impression — and decide whether to call, visit, or enrol.

Client

Coral Minds — Coaching Institute

Industry

Education · Coaching

Scope

Website design, development and foundational SEO

Contribution

Design & Development — personally handled by the studio founder

The Brief

Coral Minds is a coaching institute where the website is often the first real impression — before a parent calls, before a student visits. If the site feels outdated or unclear on a phone, the enrolment conversation is already harder than it needs to be. The brief was simple: build a website that presents Coral Minds properly — programmes people can understand at a glance, faculty that feels credible, clear ways to get in touch.

The Small Problems (and how we solved them)

Most design challenges on a project like this aren't dramatic. They're small, specific friction points that quietly make the site harder to use. Here are the ones we ran into, and how each was handled:

1. Too many course names in the navigation

Coral Minds offers different programmes across different streams and age groups. Listing every course in the top navigation would have created a menu bar with eight or nine items — every one competing for attention, and none of them winning.

Approach: We grouped them under a single "Programmes" link, and gave programmes their own dedicated page where each course had room to breathe — its own section, its own description, its own duration and eligibility details. The nav stays clean, and the information is there for anyone who wants it.

2. Faculty photos came in different sizes and backgrounds

The photos the institute supplied were taken at different times, in different lighting, with different crops. Laid out as-is, the faculty page would have looked like a scrapbook rather than a professional team.

Approach: Rather than ask the client to reshoot every photo, we standardised the treatment — every portrait was cropped to the same aspect ratio and placed on a consistent cream background, so the visual rhythm is preserved even though the originals differ. This is a small detail, but it makes the page feel like it was designed as one thing.

3. Parents kept calling to ask about fees

One of the most common phone questions the institute received was about fee structure and batch timings. It wasn't on the old site at all, which meant the reception was spending time answering questions the website could have answered itself.

Approach: We added a dedicated "Fees & Batches" page with a clean, readable table for each programme. Fee amounts for variable courses are marked "on enquiry," but base structure, batch timings, and admission windows are all clearly listed. This isn't a feature anyone will compliment — but it's the feature that quietly reduces friction.

4. The enquiry form lived only at the bottom of the contact page

On mobile, a visitor who decided to enquire had to scroll to the bottom of a long contact page to reach the form. That's a lot of scrolling for someone who has already made the decision to reach out.

Approach: We added a persistent "Enquire" call-to-action that stays within reach on mobile — visible but not intrusive, so the enquiry pathway is never more than one tap away, regardless of where the visitor is on the page.

The Design Approach Overall

Two principles shaped the look and feel:

Read well on a phone before reading well on a desktop. Coaching websites get most of their first visits on mobile. Every page was designed at a small width first, then expanded. Paragraphs are kept short, headings do the heavy lifting, and line lengths stay comfortable at any screen size.

Warm, not corporate. Education brands often drift into either corporate blue or childlike brights. Neither felt right for Coral Minds. We used a warm cream base, a soft serif for headings to feel approachable, and a muted accent colour for emphasis — a tone that works for both parents and students.

The Solution

The finished site is a multi-page website with a clear structure:

  • A welcoming homepage establishing what Coral Minds does and who it serves
  • A programmes page with each course as its own section
  • A faculty page with consistent portrait treatment
  • A fees and batches page with clear tabular information
  • An about page that tells the institute's story without over-editorialising
  • A contact page with email, phone and WhatsApp pathways
  • A mobile-first build with a persistent enquiry CTA

Key Features

  • Fully responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop
  • Semantic HTML and clean structure
  • Foundation SEO — clean URLs, metadata, heading hierarchy, alt text, sitemap, robots.txt
  • Google Search Console setup
  • Contact pathways tested end-to-end
  • Optimised images and lightweight build for fast mobile loading

Outcome

The website was completed and launched. Coral Minds now has a digital presence that reflects the quality of its teaching — a site that reads clearly on any device, answers common questions, and gives parents a clear way to get in touch. We don't publish traffic or enrolment metrics here — our role is to build well, and the outcomes belong to the client.
